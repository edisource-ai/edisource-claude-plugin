# Edisource

**Your newsroom's drafts, templates, and feeds, now inside Claude.**

Edisource is the AI-powered publishing platform built for journalists, editors, and content strategists. Your team uses it to watch beats, write in your house style, and get stories ready to publish. The Edisource plugin brings that workspace into Claude. You can ask for what you need in plain language and Claude does the work in your Edisource account, with you approving each step.

Connect the Edisource account you already have and pick up where your desk left off.

## What you can do

### Find and shape drafts

- **Search your drafts and folders.** Ask for "my most recent draft" or "the piece on the FTC ruling," and Claude opens it for you.
- **Start new drafts.** Claude creates a draft with the title you choose and files it in the folder you name. If that folder doesn't exist yet, Claude creates it too.
- **Make precise edits.** Ask for a TL;DR at the top, a tighter lede, or the removal of a stray line. Claude changes only the parts you asked about and leaves the rest of the story alone.
- **You keep the final say.** Every edit waits in Content Studio as a suggested change until you or a teammate accepts or rejects it. Nothing goes final without an editor.

### Build templates your whole newsroom can run

Templates are your newsroom's repeatable assignments. Each one combines a story form, a voice, and a set of instructions, so you get the same kind of piece every time.

- **Browse and open** your organization's template library.
- **Draft a new template with Claude.** Describe the job, such as "a weekly regulation briefing for compliance readers," and Claude writes a first version beside the chat. Refine it together, and save it to Edisource when you're happy with it.
- **Fine-tune existing templates.** Claude makes targeted wording changes without rewriting the whole template. It can also update a template's settings, such as the model it uses, the tools it can draw on, and the inputs writers fill in.
- **Protect your publishing fields.** If a change would affect the headline, deck, tags, or category fields your CMS expects, Claude tells you first.

### Run a template on fresh source material

- Give Claude a link or paste in your notes, and it runs the template you choose.
- Claude keeps you posted while the piece generates, then tells you when the new draft is ready in Content Studio.
- Claude works only from the source material you provide. It never makes up facts to fill gaps, and it never publishes a generated draft unless you ask.

### Stay on top of your beat with Discover

- **See your feeds**, and whether each one is private to you or shared with your organization.
- **Get a quick briefing.** Ask what came in on a feed this week, and Claude summarizes the items the feed collected over the last seven days, or over any time window you choose.
- **Set up new feeds** for a topic, a company, or a regulator. New feeds stay private to you unless you ask to share them with the team.
- **Update feeds** with new keywords, a new name, or different sharing.

### Check your archive and publish, when your team allows it

- **Search your CMS** to see what your publication has already run on a topic.
- **Publish a finished draft** to your connected CMS. Publishing is limited to organization admins and only works when your team has turned it on. Claude says a story is published only after Edisource confirms it.

## Your team stays in control

- **Works within your organization.** Claude works only in the Edisource organizations you already belong to. If you're on more than one, switch between them at any time.
- **Plain-language approvals.** Before each action, Claude tells you in everyday words what it's about to do, such as "Open Weekly briefing" or "Add a TL;DR to the top of this draft."
- **Admins set the boundaries.** An organization admin can turn any capability on or off in **Edisource Settings → Claude Plugin**. If something is switched off, Claude tells you and stops. It doesn't look for a workaround.
- **Edits are suggestions, not decisions.** Draft changes wait in Content Studio for an editor to review.

## Privacy and data

- The plugin talks only to Edisource, at **https://claude-plugin.edisource.co**. It doesn't send your content anywhere else.
- The plugin never asks for your CMS password. Your organization's CMS connection is set up and kept inside Edisource by your admins.
- Everything Claude does happens through your own Edisource account, with the same access you already have.

## Getting started

1. **Install the Edisource plugin** in Claude.
2. **Sign in with your Edisource account.** Edisource is built for publishing teams, so use the email address your organization invited. If you can't sign in, ask your Edisource administrator to check your invite.
3. **Choose your organization** if you belong to more than one.
4. **Ask for something useful.** For example:
   - "Open my most recent draft and add a two-sentence TL;DR at the top."
   - "What came in on my AI regulation feed this week?"
   - "Run our News Article template on this link."
   - "Help me draft a template for new-product announcements."
   - "Create a private feed tracking FDA food additive rulings."
